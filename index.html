Why nuts and dried fruit

Small handful, real nutrition.

A daily portion fits easily into a balanced diet. Here is what they are known for.

Heart health

Walnuts and almonds supply unsaturated fats that support a healthy heart as part of a balanced diet.

Brain function

Walnuts are rich in omega-3 (ALA), and nuts also provide vitamin E, both linked to normal brain function.

Immunity

Cashews and almonds contain zinc and vitamin E, nutrients that contribute to the normal function of the immune system.

Steady energy

Dates and raisins give natural sugars and fiber, while nuts add protein and fats for longer-lasting energy.

Skin care

Almonds are a source of vitamin E, which contributes to the protection of cells from oxidative stress.

General nutrition information, not medical advice. If you have a nut allergy or a medical condition, check with your doctor first.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How long do your nuts and dried fruits stay fresh?

Unopened, most of our products keep well for several months when stored properly. The best-before date is printed on every pack.

How should I store them?

Keep packs sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. After opening, move them to an airtight jar. For longer storage in warm weather, the fridge works well for nuts.

Where do you deliver and how fast?

We dispatch orders quickly and deliver to your door. Message us your location on WhatsApp and we will confirm the delivery time and charge.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes. We offer larger packs, custom gift boxes and event orders. Send us the quantity and date and we will prepare a quote.

How do you keep quality consistent?

Each batch is graded, inspected and sorted before packing. If anything in your order is not right, tell us and we will make it right.
